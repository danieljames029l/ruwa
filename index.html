Background

Why RUWA exists

Since the outbreak of conflict and economic hardship in South Sudan, many women from Rumbek and the greater Lakes region have relocated to Juba seeking safety, education, and economic opportunities. They continue to face limited access to education, unemployment, gender-based violence, and weak representation in decision-making spaces.

The Rumbek Women's Association was formed in response — bringing together women of diverse backgrounds to build solidarity, support livelihoods, and promote peace and reconciliation among communities. RUWA aligns with national priorities set out in South Sudan's National Gender Policy and the Women, Peace and Security Agenda.

“A platform for women to voice their concerns, share experiences, and collectively address the challenges affecting their livelihoods and wellbeing — in both urban and rural contexts.” RUWA Concept Note, Introduction

Our Mandate

Five objectives guide every program

§3.1

Economic empowerment

Skills development, entrepreneurship, access to financial services, and livelihood projects.

§3.2

Peace & reconciliation

Among communities from Lakes State living in Juba and beyond.

§3.3

Leadership & governance

Enhancing women's participation at community and national levels.

§3.4

Gender equality

Advocacy and protection of women and girls from GBV and all forms of violence.

§3.5

Social solidarity

Cultural preservation, mentorship, and mutual support among Rumbek women.

Strategic Interventions

Four pillars of the work

Economic Empowerment

  • Vocational training in tailoring, catering, agribusiness, and small-scale trade
  • A women's revolving savings and credit scheme
  • Market linkage support for women entrepreneurs

Peacebuilding & Reconciliation

  • Community dialogues and women's peace forums
  • Intergenerational storytelling for peace and cultural unity
  • Engagement with youth and men on peaceful coexistence

Gender Advocacy & Protection

  • Awareness campaigns on rights, GBV prevention, and access to justice
  • Partnerships with legal aid and psychosocial support providers
  • Policy dialogue and representation in local governance

Education & Capacity Development

  • Literacy and numeracy programs for women and girls
  • Scholarships and mentorship for young women leaders
  • Institutional strengthening and leadership training

II

The Council

Meets quarterly
69members

3 members nominated per Payam, equally representing Rumbek East, Centre, and North counties.

Can convene an extraordinary session at the Chair's request.

III

General Assembly

Convenes annually
115delegates

5 delegates nominated per Payam by their respective counties.

An extraordinary General Assembly may be called by the Chair with endorsement of at least one-third of Council members.

Membership categories

Ordinary

Open to eligible women from Rumbek Centre, East, and North counties residing in Juba.

Associate

Women of Rumbek serving in Parliament or Executive positions, and those married outside the three counties.

Honorary

Granted in recognition of significant contributions to the liberation struggle, nation-building, and women's empowerment — open to women of any background who share RUWA's vision.
